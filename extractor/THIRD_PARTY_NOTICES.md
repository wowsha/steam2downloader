https://github.com/dr3murr/steam2-winfsp

That project is licensed under **LGPL-3.0-or-later**. This repository fetches a pinned commit of its decoder sources at configure time; it does not copy Valve game payloads into this repository.

The upstream project's third-party notice attributes its depot-key table to the TeraRelease Steam2 extractor source and documents that provenance there.
